# Home

Status: Proposed; planning only. Parent: [Web rebuild plan](../README.md).
Route/state: /.

## Job

Help a new visitor understand Caller Whale, see credible product proof and choose the right next step.

## Section plan

1. Clear product promise and one primary CTA matched to invitation/waitlist availability.
2. Real desktop product capture with a sample-workspace caption when appropriate.
3. Three-step workflow from list import to follow-up; distinguish shipped from planned.
4. Five feature summaries linking into Features.
5. Honest product fit and limitations; pricing preview without invented savings.
6. Short FAQ and final relevant CTA.

## Actions and behavior

Primary: request access or join waitlist, chosen by the actual launch policy. Secondary: explore features. Signed-in customers get a clear Open dashboard action.

## Layout and design

Keep the existing useful product narrative and captures; remove repetitive CTAs and competing hero buttons. On mobile put headline/action before the image, and turn feature grids into a readable sequence.
Apply [shared experience rules](../01-experience.md), including light/dark, keyboard access and responsive review.

## Discovery and data protection

Public/indexable, canonical home URL and sitemap entry. Product/schema claims require visible evidence.
Apply [discovery](../02-discovery.md) and [security](../03-security.md) contracts.

## Page acceptance

Visitor can explain product, readiness and next action after the first screen. No fake testimonials, placeholder metrics or unlabeled demo screens. Page remains useful without JS.
Record desktop/mobile light/dark evidence and loading, empty, success and failure states as applicable. Mark unsupported states explicitly rather than silently omit them.
