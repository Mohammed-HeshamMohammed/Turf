# Landing web preferences

The homepage should show the app only once or twice at most. Keep one main product screenshot by default; use the remaining sections for the business story rather than repeating app captures or copying the desktop UI.

Use custom visual storytelling for the benefits, not text-only replacement sections: architectural/property illustrations should explain scattered data becoming an ordered outreach list, easier call assistance, and clear follow-ups. Keep these marketing concepts distinct from actual app screenshots and be honest about preview-only capabilities.
