# Data Processing Addendum

Version 2026-11-10. This Addendum is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms) between Caller Whale ("we", the **Provider**) and the customer ("you", the **Customer**). It applies to personal information in Customer Data: the leads, lists, notes, call records and other content you add to Caller Whale ("Customer Personal Information"). If there is a conflict between this Addendum and the Terms about that information, this Addendum wins.

## 1. Roles

You decide why and how Customer Personal Information is processed (you are the **business** or **controller**). We process it on your behalf (we are your **service provider**, **contractor** or **processor**), as these laws use those words, including the California Consumer Privacy Act and the other US state privacy laws that apply.

## 2. What we may do with it

1. We process Customer Personal Information only to provide the service to you, on your documented instructions (the Terms, your use of the service and your settings), and as the law requires. We tell you if we think an instruction breaks the law.
2. **Service improvement.** You allow us to keep and use a **learning copy** as described in section 3, and nothing else beyond the service, security, fraud prevention and legal compliance.
3. We do not **sell** or **share** (for cross-context behavioural advertising) Customer Personal Information. We do not retain, use or disclose it outside our direct business relationship with you, or for any purpose other than the business purposes in this Addendum. We do not combine it with personal information from other sources, except as the law allows a service provider to do for the permitted purposes.
4. We give you the same level of privacy protection the law requires of you, and tell you if we can no longer meet our obligations. You may take reasonable steps to stop and fix unauthorised use.

## 3. The learning copy (service improvement)

1. **What it is.** For each lead you import, we keep: a keyed hash of the phone number and of the email (the key is different for each workspace, so the same number has a different hash in each); the kind of name (person or company); city, state, ZIP; whether an address, a unit and a location were present; how complete the row was; a fixed list of property facts; and the flags your team gives the lead (stage, verdict, call outcome, do not call, priority, interested). It holds no name, phone number, email, street address, unit, coordinates, free text or recording.
2. **Purpose and limits.** The learning copy is used only to improve the service for **your workspace**, for example the order and timing of your calls. We do not use it to serve any other customer, do not match it across customers, do not sell, license or share it, and do not use it for advertising or for decisions about a person's credit, insurance, employment, housing or similar eligibility.
3. **Pseudonymised, not anonymous.** The hashes are personal information. We keep them in a separate database that our CRM service cannot read, with the key held by named owners only.
4. **Control.** Your administrators can switch the learning copy off and erase what was kept at any time (Settings, workspace). A person can ask us to remove their phone or email at [Remove my information](/remove-my-information); we delete it in every workspace and do not add it again.
5. **Deletion.** We delete the learning copy after 2 years; of a list when you delete the list; and of the workspace when you ask us to close it.
6. **Aggregates.** We may keep counts that identify no person and no workspace, and use them to improve the service for all customers. We will tell you before we use anything else across customers.

## 4. Your responsibilities

You confirm that you have the right to give us Customer Personal Information and to give the instructions in this Addendum, that you have given the notices and obtained the consents the law requires for the people in your lists, and that you will not upload the categories the Terms forbid. You are responsible for your use of lists in calls, texts and other outreach and for suppression of people who opt out.

## 5. Confidentiality and staff

People who can reach Customer Personal Information are bound to confidentiality and have access only as their work needs.

## 6. Security

We use measures that fit the risk, including: encryption in transit; access limited by role and by a second step for staff; separate databases for sign-in, CRM data and the learning copy; row-level security so a workspace sees only its own records; logs written so that they carry no list values; [BACKUP POLICY TO CONFIRM]; and a written breach response plan. We review these measures regularly.

## 7. Subprocessors

You allow us to use the providers listed in our [Privacy Notice](/privacy) (hosting, identity, bot protection, network protection, payments, email delivery, the website assistant and GIF search) to the extent they handle Customer Personal Information. We bind them by contract to protect it. We will tell you of a new subprocessor that handles Customer Personal Information, and you may object on reasonable grounds.

## 8. Requests from people

We tell you promptly if a person asks us about their information in your lists, and we help you answer, including by deleting or exporting it. People may ask us directly to remove their learning copy.

## 9. Breach

We tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours of confirming it, if Customer Personal Information in our care is accessed or disclosed without authority. We tell you what happened, what is affected, what we are doing, and what you may need to do.

## 10. Assessments and audits

We give you the information you reasonably need to show we follow this Addendum, and help with data protection assessments. Once a year, and after a breach, you may ask for a written account of our measures, or, with notice and under confidentiality, an audit of them by you or an independent assessor.

## 11. Return and deletion

When the service ends, or when you ask, we return your Customer Data in a common format and delete it and its learning copy within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.
