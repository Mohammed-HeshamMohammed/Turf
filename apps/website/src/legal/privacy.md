## Two kinds of people

**Our customers and visitors.** You use our site or service. We are the business responsible for the information in section 1.

**People in our customers' lists.** Businesses that use Caller Whale add lists of people (leads) to their workspaces. For that data, the customer decides why it is used and we act on its behalf, as its service provider (processor). If you are in a customer's list and want to see, change or delete your details there, ask that business. Section 3 explains the one copy we keep for ourselves, and how to remove it.

## 2. Customer Data (the lists and records customers add)

Leads, lists, notes, calls and tasks that customers add are theirs. We process them to provide the service: store them, sync them to the customer's team, import lists, show reports. We do not use them for anything else except as section 3 says.

## 3. The learning copy

After a list is added to a workspace, Caller Whale keeps a scrambled copy of some details, **only to improve the service for that workspace**, such as the order and timing of calls.

- **Kept:** the phone number and email only as one-way codes made with a key that is different for every workspace (so the same number looks different in each); whether the name is a person or a company; city, state and ZIP; a few property facts (such as bedrooms or year built); whether an address was present; and the flags the customer's team gives the lead (stage, verdict, call outcome, do not call).
- **Never kept:** the name, phone number, email, street address, unit, coordinates, notes or call recordings.
- **Never sold, shared or licensed.** It is not given to other customers or to anyone else. It is not used for advertising, to build profiles of you for others, or to decide credit, insurance, jobs or housing.
- **Deleted** after 2 years, when the customer deletes the list, when the customer switches it off, or when you ask.

**Remove my information.** Give a phone number or an email address at [Remove my information](/remove-my-information). We delete that person's learning copy in every workspace and remember the request so later lists do not add it again. We do not keep what you typed. This does not remove you from a business's own list: contact that business, and add your number to the National Do Not Call Registry (donotcall.gov) to stop sales calls.

We use providers to run Caller Whale. They may handle information only to provide their service to us: hosting (our server host), sign-in and identity (Google Firebase Authentication), bot protection (Google reCAPTCHA), network protection (Cloudflare), payments (Paddle), email delivery, the website assistant (OpenRouter and the AI model it uses), GIF search in the chat (GIPHY), and the code host (GitHub, which does not receive customer data). We may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect people and the service.

## 5. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia and other states), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to get a copy, and to opt out of sale, sharing for advertising and profiling. **We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.** To use a right, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] or use the [contact form](/contact). We may need to confirm who you are. We will not treat you worse for using a right. If we refuse, you may appeal by writing to us, and you may complain to your state's attorney general.

## 6. Security

Information is encrypted in transit; sign-in offers a second step; the learning copy lives in its own database that our CRM service cannot read; access is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 7. Children

Caller Whale is for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
