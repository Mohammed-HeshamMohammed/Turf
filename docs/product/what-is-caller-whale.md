# What is Caller Whale

**Caller Whale is a place for real-estate and calling teams, with a dialer built in and the easiest workflow, so a team gets the
most done.**

A calling team usually works across many tools: a spreadsheet of leads, a separate dialer, a CRM, a texting app and a chat for the
team. Caller Whale puts all of that in one place. A team brings in its lists, calls and texts the leads from inside the app, writes
down what happened, and every lead keeps an owner and a next step.

## The promise

| What the team gets | How |
| --- | --- |
| **More calls per hour** | Auto dialing and several lines at once, with as few clicks as possible between calls. |
| **Nothing falls through** | Every lead has an owner and a next step with a date. Callbacks and follow-ups come back on time. |
| **The manager sees everything** | Who is calling right now, what happened on each call, and the team's numbers. |
| **All in one place** | List, dialer, texts, CRM and team chat together. No switching between apps. |

## The apps

| App | Who uses it most |
| --- | --- |
| **Desktop app** (Windows, Mac later) | Callers working a full day of calls. |
| **Web app** (app.callerwhale.com) | Anyone, from any computer, without installing anything. |
| **Mobile app** (iOS and Android) | Calls, texts and follow-ups away from the desk. |

All three use the same account and the same leads.

## The name

Caller Whale is a play on *killer whale*: the orca is the brand animal, and "caller" is the work. Always two words, capital C and W.
"Turf" is the old project name, used only inside the code.
