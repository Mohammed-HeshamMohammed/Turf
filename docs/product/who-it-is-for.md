# Who it is for

## The main audience: calling teams and agencies

Teams whose daily work is calling property owners and other leads: a manager and a group of callers, often with researchers who
prepare the lists and closers who take a good lead forward. Caller Whale is built first for them. When a choice has to be made,
it is made for the calling team.

What they need from Caller Whale:

- Callers: to call fast, see the lead's details and history on the call, and write the result in one click.
- Researchers: to bring lists in, clean them and find the best number to call.
- Closers: to pick up a lead that is ready, with everything said before.
- Managers: to share out the leads, see who is doing what, and watch the numbers.

## Everyone else

Caller Whale also serves:

| Who | How they use it |
| --- | --- |
| Real-estate investors and wholesalers | Calling owners from their own lists to find deals. |
| Solo operators | One person doing all of it, without a team-sized screen in the way. |
| Realtors and agents | Prospecting sellers and keeping follow-ups going. |
| Other call centers | Any team that calls leads from lists, not only real estate. |
| Clients and partners | Seeing only the leads and notes a team chooses to share with them. |
